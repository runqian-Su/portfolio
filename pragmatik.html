Research toward a persistent persona agent organizes authority, event facts, historical influence and recovery within separate domains. Current validation covers bounded protocols and local tendencies; real-world initiative remains unvalidated.

From Experience to Traceable Local Tendencies

Immediate memory effects, outcome/time-driven historical change and stable cross-event tendencies are distinct mechanisms. W10 slowly accumulates eligible independent outcomes into local tendencies, then projects them into new events.

W10 controlled-history comparison: different valid experiences change the choice for the same probe. Physical action payloads remain identical; different choices do not establish different real-world effects.

Evidence: historical comparisons in W10-validation and stage alignment; same probe for consistent and mixed histories.

ACCUMULATION RULES

Slow Updates from Independent Evidence

Levels advance only after enough aligned eligible experiences meet spacing and span requirements. Duplicate, invalid and Unknown outcomes are ineligible.

CHANNEL BOUNDARIES

Control Memory & Tendencies Separately

Cross-comparisons help explain channel effects. Saturation and interaction prevent assuming universal additivity.

Only a local tendency in one registered dimension and scope is validated; not a full personality structure or general personality learning.

SERA / PERSISTENT EXECUTIONSERA · W9 / W10

Constraining Actions through Resources, Authority and Recovery

Valid resource state left by prior events changes admission for an identical later event. Rechecking current conditions after waiting differs from recovering a consumed intention.

Numbers are offline SERA experiment parameters. Insufficient resources retain the event in waiting. A new Continue may use current conditions; recovery cannot refresh a committed decision using the latest tendency.

Evidence: W9 resource-waiting comparison, W10-validation combined checks and W10-handoff recovery protocol.

CONTINUATION RULES

Waiting Is Not a New Action

Explicit time advances resource recovery; facts, authority and history are rechecked before continuing. Waiting consumes no use authorization, sends no action and reinforces no experience.

RECOVERY RULES

Unknown Outcomes Remain Unknown

Unknown actions are neither resent nor given fabricated Outcomes. Consumed or applied intentions recover under their original revision, prefix and watermark.

Real models/tools, dynamic resolution, competing events, multidimensional personality and emotional dynamics remain unvalidated. Arbitrary external actions have no exactly-once guarantee.

Feedback

Prior consumption affects the next event through valid state.

Gating

Resources and authority jointly constrain admission; waiting has explicit state.

Recovery

Retain decisions and receipts; do not retry unknown results as failures.

CONCEPT WORLD / STRUCTURED COGNITIONResearch Positioning · Numerical and Formal Models

Concept World | Inspectable Meaning and Transformations

Numerical concept states raise further questions: what is computed, which transformations are legal, and what evidence supports results? Concept World studies finite local concept worlds humans and AI can operate on together.

EXISTING NUMERICAL PROTOTYPE / META MATH

Legacy Ideon

Complex positions, velocities, wave packets and graph relations carry state evolution, candidate competition and path selection.

Open questions: how do numerical changes correspond to domain meaning, and why would internal stability support external conclusions?

REFRAMED FORMAL RESEARCH / CONCEPT WORLD

Ideon / Concept Particle

Constructs a concept as an addressable, composable typed generative term under a specific goal, perspective and scale.

Studies types, structural interfaces, legal operations, and how evidence and derivation jointly constrain computation.

This reframes the research question. Formal Ideons do not automatically inherit legacy C⁵ states or dynamics; implementation compatibility and automatic migration are not promised.

01 / What Are the Objects?

Explicit identity, type, scale and scope; semantic relations; structural interfaces retained by composite objects.

02 / How Do They Change?

Transformations declare inputs, preconditions, postconditions and invariants; unknown and failure states are explicit.

03 / Why Accept a Result?

Trace sources, assumptions, evidence and derivation; formal legality is followed by domain validation.

“Finite and Local”: Each model states its goal, viewpoint, scale and scope, retaining unresolved relations and open possibilities.

Sources: Concept-World README; formal Ideon research foundations (28 Jul 2026); preliminary bounded typed-generative-term hypothesis (29 Jul 2026); technical lineage notes.

Research Definition / Preliminary Hypothesis · Jul 2026

From Particles to Local Worlds

Ideon is the current construction starting point. Particles, relations, states and context form a Configuration: a local structure for shared operations.

04 / Why a Shared Foundation?

Structural analysis, concept construction and action modeling need compatible types and operations, reducing ambiguity from ad hoc natural-language interpretation.

05 / Current Status

Object positioning, formal requirements and mathematical candidates exist. A minimal type system, operator execution and cross-tool vertical slice still need implementation and validation.

Structural interfaces define composition; permission boundaries define who may act. These require separate definitions. Formal well-formedness does not prove real-world truth.
